Built Private, On Purpose

Your time is yours.

Wildly Offline runs on Apple's Screen Time tools, and your detailed usage never leaves your phone. To run accounts and friends we store a small profile and only what you choose to share with friends. No ads, no tracking, no selling your data. You can delete your account and all your data inside the app. Your hours belong to you, and Wildly Offline is built that way on purpose.

Common Questions

Answers to the
things people ask.

Wildly Offline is an iPhone app for people 13 and older that helps you spend less time on your phone. You start a session, your distracting apps lock, and every phone-free minute earns coins. Coins buy gear for your character, and you can compare phone-free time with friends for trophies.
Download Wildly Offline from the App Store, open it, confirm you are 13 or older, and continue with Apple or Google. Pick a name and username, then allow Screen Time access when asked. Tap Start session on Home and choose which apps stay open.
Wildly Offline is built on Apple's Screen Time tools. It needs that permission to lock the apps you choose during a session and to show your own insights. Apple keeps the details on your phone: Wildly Offline can't read your usage per app and never sends it anywhere.
Your per-app screen time never leaves your phone. To run accounts and friends we store your name, username, profile and the stats you share with friends, plus an optional photo. Only friends you accept can see them. No ads, no tracking, no selling your data. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.
Tap Start session and every app locks except the ones you allow, such as Messages, Phone and Maps. Social apps and games always stay locked. You earn coins for each phone-free minute, and you can end a session any time from Home.
No. The portal is an optional Bluetooth accessory: place your figure on it to start a session and lift it to end one. Without a portal, the Start button does the same job.
In the app, open the Friends tab, then Account, then Delete account. You sign in once more to confirm, and your profile, photo, stats, friend connections, messages and account are removed from our servers. Deleting the app on its own does not delete your online account. If you can't open the app, email kizzunaconnect@gmail.com from the address on your account.
