Legal · Privacy

Your time is
yours.

Your detailed screen time stays on your iPhone. We don't run ads or tracking, we don't sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything with it inside the app.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

Wildly Offline helps you spend less time on your phone. Your detailed screen time stays on your iPhone. To run accounts and friends, we store a small profile and the progress you choose to share with friends. We don't run ads or tracking, and we don't sell your data.

Who we are

Wildly Offline is published by Disconnected Education LLC ("Wildly Offline", "we"). Contact: kizzunaconnect@gmail.com.

Who can use Wildly Offline

Wildly Offline is for people aged 13 and over. When you create an account you confirm you are 13 or older. If you are under 13, please don't use the app. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it.

What stays on your iPhone

Screen Time details. Wildly Offline uses Apple's Screen Time tools (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity). Your usage per app, pickups, notifications and the list of apps you choose to lock stay on your device. Apple's frameworks don't let us read them, and we never send them to a server.

Your settings and progress (sleep window, allowed apps, coins, gear and session history) are stored on your device.

The Bluetooth portal. If you pair a Wildly Offline portal, the app talks to it over Bluetooth on your phone. Nothing from that connection is sent to us.

What we store on our servers when you make an account

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to run accounts and friends. When you create an account, we store:

  • Account details: a unique account ID, and your name or email as given by Sign in with Apple or Google. We only ask Apple for your name, not your email.
  • Your profile: display name, username, your figure and its gear, your time zone, and an optional profile photo (a small JPEG).
  • What your friends see: your phone-free minutes per day, whether a session is running, your last session, streak, trophies and league.
  • Optional screen time total: only if you turn on "Share screen time" in Account. It is a daily total, never per app. It is off by default and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Messages and cheers: preset reactions and emoji-only messages you send to friends.
  • Friend connections and any blocks.
  • Reports: if you report a photo, we keep who reported, who was reported and when, so we can review it.

We use this only to run Wildly Offline: show you and your accepted friends each other's progress, let you find friends by username, and keep your account working. Only people you have accepted as friends can see your stats and photo.

What we don't do

No advertising. No tracking across other apps or websites. No analytics or crash-reporting tools. No selling or renting your data. No data brokers.

Who processes your data

Google (Firebase) stores and processes account and friends data for us under its terms. Apple and Google also process your sign-in. We don't share your data with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your account any time in the app: Friends tab, then Account, then Delete account. You sign in once more to confirm. This removes your profile, photo, stats, friend connections, messages, username and account from our servers, and ends Wildly Offline's access to your Apple sign-in. It also erases progress stored on your phone.
  • Block or report: open a friend's page to block them or report their photo.
  • Turn off sharing: Account, then Share screen time, or pause sharing your stats.
  • Screen Time access can be turned off in iOS Settings, then Screen Time.
  • If you can't open the app, email kizzunaconnect@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete your data.

Keeping data

We keep your server data until you delete your account. Reports may be kept a little longer so we can act on abuse.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and Firebase encrypts stored data. Access to your friends' data is limited by rules so that only accepted friends can read it. No system is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account in the app does the last of these; for anything else, email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Wildly Offline handles data, we will update this page and the date above, and the App Store privacy details.
